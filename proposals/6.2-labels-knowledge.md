# Proposal (AIM 6.2) — labels and knowledge

Status: **agreed and written into the specification (AIM 6.2), 2026-11-01** · Author: Michael & Claude working session · Affects: §2 (frontmatter), §3 (names, labels, references), §4–§5 (addresses, resolution), §6 (Capability, State, a new §6.11 Knowledge, Flow continuation and waiting, Trigger), §7 (edges, intents as endpoints, `consults`), §8 (no imports, provider intents), §10–§13 (diagnostics, moving, transforms, provenance)

## Where this comes from

A real owner session: Stefan Aubele (IT-Kompass) modelling Barth Industrieverpackungen in Studio, 2026-10-30. That session and five benchmark runs of it (case `5-barth`) showed two gaps in the language.

## Part 1 — labels: names stay ASCII, people read their own words

### The problem

§3 requires a facet name to match `[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_]*`. A German owner's roles and fields become `Geschaeftsfuehrerin`, `Lohnbuero`, `laengeInMm` and `ippcGewuenscht`. Every surface that shows a name shows that spelling. Splitting camel case back into words gives "Laenge in mm" and "Ippc gewuenscht". The owner said what they meant, and the model cannot keep it.

**Owner decision (2026-10-31): names stay ASCII.** They are identities: addresses, edges and file paths depend on them, and about fifty patterns in every tool read them. Instead, the owner's own spelling becomes a **label** that people see.

### Proposal

- **Facets.** Any facet may carry `### Label`, one line: the name as people say it, in any script. **It comes right after the heading, before `### Summary`** (owner 2026-10-31): §3's "the facet heading MUST be immediately followed by `### Summary`" becomes "…by `### Summary`, or by `### Label` and then `### Summary`" (a Persona used only for role and access, which needs no Summary, may likewise start with `### Label`), and §6.10 adds Label to the sub-blocks a State admits.

  ```markdown
  ## Persona: Geschaeftsfuehrerin

  ### Label

  Geschäftsführerin

  ### Role

  - Vertrieb, Angebote, Einkauf, Disposition, Fibu und Freigaben
  ```

- **Fields.** A schema field may carry the modifier `label("…")`:

  ```schema
  laengeInMm: number required label("Länge in mm")
  ippcGewuenscht: boolean label("IPPC gewünscht")
  ```

- **What a label is.**
  - It is display only. Resolution, addresses, edges and every check use the name; a label never resolves and never makes two names one.
  - Without a label, the name is shown, split into words as today.
  - A label is not unique. Two facets may share one, and tools disambiguate by name where it matters.
  - It is part of the model: an authoring tool that writes a name from the owner's words writes the label with it (Amy's Studio does: "Geschäftsführerin" → name `Geschaeftsfuehrerin`, label `Geschäftsführerin`).
- **Enum values** take a label the same way, display only: `enum(Offen, InPruefung "In Prüfung")`. People who use the app see "In Prüfung"; the value stored and checked stays `InPruefung`.
- **States** reuse the labels of the enum field they govern (§6.10). Every state spelling valid today stays valid.
- **Intents need no label.** An intent file's H1 is already its display name (§2, with frontmatter `display`). When a facet is promoted to an intent (§11), its label becomes the H1.
- **Not labelled.** Requirement labels (`SAME-BOX`) are keys, and owners read the statement, never the key. Namespaces and paths stay ASCII.

### Diagnostics

- Informational: a `### Label` with more than one line, or an empty one.
- Hard error: `label(...)` that is not one quoted string; an enum value label that is not one quoted string after its value.

## Part 2 — Knowledge: what the work consults, apart from what it calls

### The problem

§6.8 makes a Capability "the kind of outside service **or information source** the model relies on". Barth's models and the review of real workspaces show three different things under that one word:

| What it is | Examples | What the running work does with it |
|---|---|---|
| A system the business runs on | Odoo, Datev, Excel lists, Zoho | Reads and writes records through operations |
| A service | sending e-mail, making a PDF, a payment provider, a web API, an MCP server | Calls an operation and gets a typed result |
| A body of knowledge | a handbook, a regulation, a supplier's certificate rules, a wiki, a document collection | **Consults** it: reads, searches, understands, to decide well |

The first two are the same kind of thing to the language: a boundary with typed operations. The third is not. Sending an e-mail and reading the ISPM-15 handbook are not the same commitment. The model cannot say "this decision is made with the handbook", so nobody can ask which work depends on it or notice that it is missing.

**Owner principle (2026-10-31):** a Capability is what the work **uses when it runs**, not what Amy reads while modelling. Knowledge in that sense is the same: something the running work consults, possibly behind an API. What Amy reads while modelling stays the product's Knowledge feature and is not AIM.

### Proposal

**A new facet, `Knowledge`.** A body of knowledge the running work consults to understand or decide, named by what it holds, never by where it is hosted.

| Sub-block | Required | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| `### Summary` | yes | What it holds, and what it is the reference for. |
| `### Source` | no | Where it lives, in words: a document collection, a wiki space, a regulation. Never credentials or a provider's address. |
| `### Bindings` | no | The realization that answers it: a connector, a document store, an API (§9). |

```markdown
## Knowledge: HolzNachweise

### Label

Holz-Nachweise

### Summary

Welche Nachweise ein Lieferant für welches Holz liefern muss: Herkunft für EUDR bei jedem Material, HT-Nachweis nur bei Vollholz.

### Source

Die Lieferantenvereinbarungen und die ISPM-15-Unterlagen der Firma.
```

**A new verb, `consults`.** It goes from a contract, flow or view to a knowledge facet: the operation reads or searches that knowledge while it runs, for example an agent answering a supplier question. It derives no inverse.

It passes the extension policy (§7):
1. It relates existing node types to the new one.
2. `invokes` means calling an operation and `reads` means a Record; neither says "consults text to decide".
3. It enables a query (which work depends on this knowledge) and diagnostics (below).
4. The need is demonstrated in Barth and in agentic processes.
5. Its endpoints, its absence of an inverse and its diagnostics are given here.

**The rule that keeps the model the authority.** Knowledge informs; it never decides for the model. A rule the work MUST follow is stated in the model, as a requirement, an `Expects` or a `Decides` outcome, even when it comes from the knowledge. "Proof of origin always; HT certificate only for solid wood" is a statement of B1, B2 and B3, or one company rule. It is not just "see the certificate rules". The Knowledge facet says where the background is, so the work can consult it for what the model does not state.

**Supplied material.** The content of a Knowledge facet is supplied material (§8): it is never written into `.aim`. An intent that consults knowledge owes it supplied, like a Record read but never written.

**Knowledge is sufficient on its own.** Consulting Knowledge satisfies the rule that outside information is declared: it requires no separate Capability and no intermediate Record. §6.2 ("an operation acquiring outside information MUST declare the Capability it consults") becomes "…MUST declare the Capability it invokes or the Knowledge it consults"; §6.8's route for document-shaped information through a collect Contract and a Record applies to a Capability, not to Knowledge; §8's "a required external service or information source is a Capability facet" names Knowledge beside it. `consults` counts as the basis of a decision wherever the language asks what a decision rests on.

**Knowledge is read, not copied.** Knowledge may be searched, summarized and interpreted; its contents do not override the model's commitments. §8's "consumers MUST use supplied material verbatim" applies to copy, seed data and media, not to consulted Knowledge.

**Capability stays as it is,** reworded to "an outside system or service the work calls through operations". "Information source" moves to Knowledge where the information is consulted as text, and stays a Capability where it is a typed answer (`FetchRates(currency) -> rates`).

**§6's count** becomes eleven facets.

**An example of use**, on the operation where the knowledge is used:

```markdown
## Contract: AnswerSupplierQuestion

### Summary

Answers a supplier's question about the certificates Barth needs for their material.

### Ensures

- The answer uses the applicable certificate rules — [consults](aim:#Knowledge:HolzNachweise)
- The question and the answer are kept — [mutates](aim:#Record:SupplierQuestion)
```

### Diagnostics

- Hard error: `consults` to anything that is not a Knowledge facet, or from anything other than a contract, flow or view.
- Informational:
  - a Knowledge facet nothing consults;
  - a Knowledge facet with no Source and no Bindings, whose content is owed but nowhere said.

**Guidance for reviewers, not a diagnostic.** An operation that consults knowledge while its Summary says it decides, with no `Decides` or rule in the model, keeps its decision only in the text. A reviewer, human or model, should ask for the rule; no parser reads a Summary's meaning to find this.

### Decided (owner 2026-10-31, both reviews agreeing)

- The verb is **`consults`**, clearer than widening `reads`.
- **Only operations consult** (contract, flow, view), and promoted processes once F01 lands; a Persona does not. The edge sits on the step or operation where the knowledge is used.
- In Studio, Capabilities are **Services / Dienste**, one list and one name (owner: "I don't like having two names for something"). Capability gets no `kind`. GitHub, SAP, generating a PDF and sending an e-mail are all services; **an account** ("my Gmail account") is not a service but its connection: configuration under the service, never in the model.

### Decided (owner, confirmed 2026-11-01)

- **A service is named by the system the owner chose** when that choice is the owner's decision (GitHub, SAP, Odoo are services by those names); otherwise by what it offers. §6.8's "never by provider" becomes: *named by what it offers, or by the system the owner chose when that choice is the owner's decision.*
- **One Knowledge in Studio.** The Knowledge page keeps the documents (read once and searchable, or reached live through a connection, e.g. a large database); a `## Knowledge` facet is written when a process first consults something, and its Source and Bindings point there. What fulfils it is not AIM: one connection may fulfil a Knowledge facet and a service at once.

## Part 3 — promoted intents and full addresses (F01, F04) · agreed, owner 2026-11-01

Agreed so far:

- **One full address per thing, no aliases.** A facet is `<intent>#<Type>:<Name>` (`company#Persona:HR`); a promoted Record, Persona or process **is** its intent (`company.kunde`). Identity is never a title or a label, and an old address is never kept alive by guessing.
- **Promotion and moving rewrite every reference.** Promoting a facet, or moving a shared thing up to the lowest common ancestor of the intents that use it (§11), is one change to approve that repoints every reference across the workspace (§12 rule 1). An address left behind (outside the workspace) is a dangling reference, said, never guessed. Depth of an address therefore says how widely a thing is shared: what the company shares sits near the root and has a short address.
- **Edges are legal by what an intent holds**, never by `nature` (a hint only): an intent with `## Schema` is read, mutated and referenced like a Record; one with `## Role` is an actor for Authz, `plays`, `accesses` and performing work; one with `## Steps` is invoked and triggered like a process. One intent may be several (a customer who is kept and also acts). A promoted Persona MUST keep `## Role`; an intent used as an actor without one is a hard error.
- **Field references:** `ref(company.kunde#id)` is a field of the intent's own Schema (lowercase after `#`); `ref(company.kunde#Angebot.id)` a field of a Record facet inside it (capitalized name first). Both are unambiguous; `#` marks where the intent path ends, like Javadoc's `Class#member`.
- **Same-named facets are promoted together** when they are one real thing (`Record:Kunde` and `Persona:Kunde` become `company.kunde`); promoting only one leaves a probable duplicate, said.
- **No imports (F04).** AIM is not a programming language: `### Imports` is removed; full addresses replace it, and models that use imports are rewritten into full addresses by the migration on open.
- **Service intents are the exception to moving.** They live under `service.…`, outside the company's tree, and are never moved into or out of; only reading the service again changes them. Their addresses are at most two levels (service, area). The company reaches their operations through its own Capability and the §8 mapping, and their data only as a value of its shape (`part(service.sap.sales#Auftrag)`) or an external identifier — never `ref()`, which needs a row of the company's own.

- **Full everywhere** (owner 2026-11-01). Every reference is written with its intent, inside its own intent too: `[reads](aim:company#Record:Kunde)`, `ref(company#Kunde.id)`, `company#Persona:HR` in Authz. §5's "the namespace may be omitted when resolution is within the same intent" is withdrawn. The short forms (`#Record:Kunde`, `ref(Kunde.id)`) stay readable as deprecated spellings and are rewritten in full by the migration on open; tools write only the full form. A reference then reads the same in every file, and moving or promoting rewrites one spelling.

## Part 4 — the remaining consistency points (2B) · all agreed, owner 2026-11-01

### F14 — "Only in" replaces the in-state guard · agreed

`<From> → unchanged — [Contract:X]` is written like a move and is none. A State gets its own sub-block for it:

```markdown
### Moves
- Entwurf → Geprüft — [Contract:Pruefen]
- Geprüft → Freigegeben — [Contract:Freigeben]

### Only in
- Entwurf — [Contract:Bearbeiten]
- Entwurf, Geprüft — [Contract:Kommentieren]
```

- An operation under **Only in** runs only while the record is in one of those states, whatever it writes. One meaning; no composition rule with Moves.
- A move by an operation that Only in confines to other states can never happen: hard error, naming both lines.
- The reserved target `unchanged` is deprecated: still read, rewritten into Only in by the migration on open. §6.10's Form gains the sub-block (State now admits Summary, Of, States, Moves, Only in and Bindings).
- Engine: Live checks every write of an Only-in operation against its states (today only writes that leave the field unchanged).

### F10 — the step says which outcomes continue · agreed

```markdown
2. Machbarkeit prüfen — [invokes](aim:company.a1#Contract:MachbarkeitPruefen) — continues: Machbar, Sonderzuschnitt
```

- A step invoking a deciding Contract MAY end with `— continues: <Label>, <Label>`. The named outcomes go on to the next step after their own consequence; an outcome not named ends with its own consequence. Several outcomes may continue (§7.3's "one outcome" becomes "the outcomes named").
- On the step, not the Contract: the same decision may continue differently in another process.
- Needed only when ambiguous. Without it, the one outcome with no consequence of its own continues, as in 6.1; when that is not exactly one, the ambiguous-continuation note asks for `continues:` and tools never guess from labels.
- A label that is no outcome of the invoked decision is a hard error. "The next step always follows by position" (event continuation) defers to this.

### F11 — in Steps, subscribes means wait · agreed

- A `subscribes` inside a Flow's or promoted process's Steps is a wait: the case pauses at that step and continues with the next step when the Event arrives for this case.
- For this case: the Event was emitted within the case, or its Payload references the case's own record (`angebot: ref(company.a1#Angebot.id)`).
- A subscription outside Steps (a Flow or Contract that subscribes at its top) stays an entry point that starts a run, as in 6.1. Rule: in Steps, subscribes = wait; outside Steps = start.
- Waiting has no built-in limit; a deadline Trigger states one (F12b).

### F12b — a deadline belongs to its case · agreed

- A deadline Trigger is armed per case, and only its `unless` Event **of that case** disarms it. Of that case: as in F11, emitted within the case or naming the case's record in its Payload.
- The anchoring Event again in the same case restarts the timer from the new Event: one timer per case, never two.
- Any other policy (count from the first anchor, keep several timers) is stated in the Trigger's Summary, normatively; the realization follows it.

### F05 — a move never counts as approval · agreed

- Provenance stays per file. Moving an inferred piece into a confirmed intent is one change that says so ("… is not confirmed yet; moving it into … confirms it"); approving it is the confirmation, visible and deliberate. Declining leaves the piece where it was.
- Moving a confirmed piece into an inferred intent is refused as an automatic transform: confirm the destination first, or keep the piece in its own sibling facet file inside that intent, which keeps its own status.
- Nothing changes status without a person seeing it (§12 transforms; §13 confirmation per intent).

### F16 — the calendar and the clock are intent · agreed

```yaml
timezone: Europe/Berlin
calendar: DE-BY
```

- Two frontmatter keys, defined by the spec: `timezone` (an IANA zone) and `calendar` (a country or country-region code: Monday to Friday plus that region's public holidays). Set on the company root, inherited by every descendant; an intent may override either (a branch abroad).
- Every schedule and deadline counts in that time zone and, for `business-days`, that calendar. Company closures are a requirement in the owner's words, normative, and deadlines respect them.
- A `business-days` deadline with no calendar in reach, or a schedule with no time zone in reach: informational, asking for it.
- How dates are computed and stored, and the holiday tables, stay realization. §6.7's "the calendar behind business-days is realization" becomes: which calendar and time zone is intent; how it is computed is realization.
